Installation Process

Follow these steps in order to ensure your executor attaches and executes correctly.

01

Download & Antivirus

Download the executor and disable your real-time protection or add an exclusion to the folder. Most executors are flagged as "False Positives" due to how they inject into the game.

https://github.com/23Pulto/Roblox-Executor/releases
02

Launch Roblox (Web)

Open your browser and launch a Roblox game via the website. **Note:** The Microsoft Store (UWP) version is currently not supported by most executors.

03

Attach Executor

Once you are in a game, open the Nebula Executor as Administrator and click the **"Attach"** or **"Inject"** button. Wait for the confirmation message.

04

Execute Scripts

Paste your desired script into the text editor and click **"Execute"**. Your script should now be running in the game environment.
